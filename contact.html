FAQ

Are you open to research collaborations?

Yes. I’m especially interested in robust representation learning, efficient attention, and reasoning in language models. Send a short note about the idea and what you have in mind.

Do you take on students or mentees?

I mentor a small number of students each year through open-source and research programs. Tell me about your background and what you want to learn.

Can you speak at our event or reading group?

Happy to, schedule permitting. Include the date, format and audience in your message.

How quickly do you reply?

Usually within 2 to 3 working days. If it’s urgent, mention that in the subject line.
