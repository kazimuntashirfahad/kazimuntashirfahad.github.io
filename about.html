Researcher, engineer and lifelong learner. I care about AI that is rigorous in the lab and dependable in the real world.

Hello, I’m Kazi

AI researcher & ML enthusiast

I’m Kazi Fahad, a machine learning researcher working on deep learning, NLP, computer vision and representation learning. I’m fascinated by a simple question: what does it take for a model to truly understand the data it learns from?

My research spans robust representation learning, efficient attention, and reasoning in language models. Alongside papers, I build open-source tools that make ML research faster and more reproducible, used by thousands of researchers and engineers.

Outside of work I mentor students getting started in ML, write about research on my blog, and spend too much time reading papers on the train.
