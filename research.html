Research focus

Research.

I work at the intersection of deep learning, language and vision, with a simple goal: build models that understand the world well enough to be useful and trustworthy in it.

01

Deep Learning

My work in deep learning focuses on architectures and training methods that remain efficient and reliable as data grows messier and models grow larger.

Open questions

  • How can attention scale to long contexts without quadratic cost?
  • Which training signals make models robust to noisy or scarce labels?
  • How do we get calibrated uncertainty from deep networks?

Related work

Uncertainty-Guided Pseudo Labeling for Low-Resource Learning ICML 2024Efficient Sparse Attention for Long-Context Vision Transformers NeurIPS 2024Calibrated Uncertainty for Medical Image Segmentation AAAI 2022
02

Natural Language Processing

I study how language models reason, where that reasoning breaks, and how to make small models competitive, including for low-resource languages like Bangla.

Open questions

  • Can fine-tuning on intermediate thoughts improve factual consistency?
  • How well does knowledge transfer across languages with little data?
  • How do we evaluate reasoning beyond final-answer accuracy?

Related work

Enhancing LLM Reasoning via Thought-Aware Fine-Tuning ACL 2023Cross-Lingual Transfer for Low-Resource Bangla NLP EMNLP 2022
03

Computer Vision

In vision I work on recognition systems that generalize to new domains, from street scenes to medical images, with a focus on what models actually see.

Open questions

  • Why do vision models fail under distribution shift?
  • How can semantic structure guide contrastive learning?
  • What makes dense prediction transfer well from pretraining?

Related work

Semantic-Aware Contrastive Learning for Domain Generalization CVPR 2024Efficient Sparse Attention for Long-Context Vision Transformers NeurIPS 2024Self-Supervised Pretraining with Masked Feature Prediction ICLR 2023Reproducible Training Pipelines for Large-Scale Vision Models TPAMI 2023
04

Representation Learning

Good representations are the backbone of everything else. I explore self-supervised objectives that capture semantics and transfer across tasks and modalities.

Open questions

  • What should a model predict when it predicts masked content?
  • How do we measure representation quality without labels?
  • Which invariances help, and which ones hurt?

Related work

Semantic-Aware Contrastive Learning for Domain Generalization CVPR 2024Robust Representations Under Distribution Shift: A Survey arXiv 2024Self-Supervised Pretraining with Masked Feature Prediction ICLR 2023
05

MLOps & Deployment

Research only matters if it ships. I build tooling for reproducible training, evaluation and deployment so results hold up outside the lab.

Open questions

  • How do we make large training runs deterministic and auditable?
  • What should continuous evaluation look like for ML models?
  • How do we monitor models for silent failure after deployment?

Related work

Reproducible Training Pipelines for Large-Scale Vision Models TPAMI 2023
